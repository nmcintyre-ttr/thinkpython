**Covers:** Lists as sequences, mutability, list operations and methods, map/filter/reduce, deleting elements, lists and strings, objects and values, aliasing, and list arguments. The first mutable data structure in depth.

- Core topic: lists in Python 3, taught incrementally with small examples.

- Exercises range from short checks of understanding to substantial projects, most with linked solutions.
