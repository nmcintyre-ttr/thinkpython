# Chapter 17: Classes and methods — summary

**Covers:** Object-oriented features, printing objects, the init method, the str method, operator overloading, type-based dispatch, polymorphism, and interfaces. Making classes behave like built-in types.

## Key points
- Core topic: classes and methods in Python 3, taught incrementally with small examples.
- Every chapter ends with a Debugging section (general techniques and Python pitfalls) and a Glossary of terms defined at first use.
- Exercises range from short checks of understanding to substantial projects, most with linked solutions.

## What the reader takes away
- Object-oriented features, printing objects, the init method, the str method, operator overloading, type-based dispatch, polymorphism, and interfaces.
- Practical, runnable patterns rather than language trivia: Downey's stated method is short chapters, minimal jargon, and gradual build-up to avoid trap doors.
